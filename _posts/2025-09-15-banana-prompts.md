# Nano Banana Gemini Viral Prompts to bring zest in your Insta Posts
## What is Nano Banana
* Codename for a very small experimental Gemini model
* Limited multimodal/image-generation capability compared to larger Gemini variants
* Produces lower-fidelity images with simpler composition and more artifacts
* Faster inference and lower compute requirements suitable for low-resource devices
* Requires clearer, simpler prompts and more post-processing to improve outputs
